お土産omiyage: a gift you bring home

In Japan, no one returns from a journey empty-handed. They bring omiyage: a sweet from the place they visited, beautifully boxed and wrapped so it can be shared.

Goa has its cashews and its bebinca. Kaju Koi is Goa's omiyage: a crisp, delicate cookie that looks beautiful unopened, travels well and is easy to split among colleagues, family and friends.

The cookie

Goa's ingredients, made with Japanese care.

A matchbox-sized rectangle. Two thin, crisp cookies made with Goan cashew flour, pale golden at the centre and a little deeper at the rim. Between them, a thin, smooth, even layer of roasted cashew and coconut jaggery cream.

  • TasteToasty butter and roasted cashew first, then warm, caramel-like coconut jaggery. Moderately sweet, a hint of salt.
  • TextureA snap and a delicate crunch, then it melts quickly. The cream is smooth and firm, not sticky.
  • WrappedOne by one, in a matte cream wrapper with our red cashew seal.
  • EnjoyWith tea, coffee, or a quiet moment. Share one, keep one.

Our story

Cashew love, from Goa

Kaju Koi was started in Goa by Varun Modgill, who studies Japanese and runs Japanese restaurants. In Japan, nobody comes home from a trip empty-handed. You bring omiyage, a boxed sweet from the place you visited, each piece wrapped so it can be shared. Varun wanted Goa to have one.

So Kaju Koi takes the thing Goa grows best, the cashew brought here by the Portuguese over four centuries ago, and makes it into a crisp, delicate cookie. It is baked in Goa, filled with roasted cashew and coconut jaggery, and wrapped one by one.

Kaju is cashew. Koi (恋) is Japanese for love. Put together: cashew love, from Goa, with love from Japan.
